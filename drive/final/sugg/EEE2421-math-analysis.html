Tier 1 · Near-certain Induction motor — speed / slip / rotor frequency / torque

This lives in Q1(b) — or its OR — in every single one of the 7 papers. In the last four semesters running it showed up twice per paper (main and OR both numeric). If you only prepare one calculation cold, it's this one.

Two variants dominate the slot

7/7 semesters
22S23S23A24S24A25S25A

Variant A — "the 4-pole, 50 Hz" version

Given: a 4- (occasionally 5- or 6-) pole, 3-phase motor on a 50 Hz supply. Find: synchronous/stator field speed, plus rotor speed and rotor-current frequency at 2–3 stated slip values, plus frequency at standstill.

Word-for-word identical in Spring 2025 Q1(b) and Autumn 2025 Q1(b). Close variants in Spring 2022 Q1(d) and Autumn 2024 Q2(a)-OR.

Variant B — the "208 V / 10 hp / Y-connected" classic

Given: 208-V, 10-hp, four-pole, 60-Hz, Y-connected motor, full-load slip = 5%. Find: synchronous speed, rotor speed, rotor frequency, shaft torque — this exact four-part ask, straight out of a standard machines textbook.

Autumn 2023 Q1(c) (50 Hz version), Spring 2024 Q1(b)-OR, and Autumn 2025 Q1(b)-OR — three appearances, same structure.
Tier 2 · High probability Group-B statistics & error propagation

The two workhorse numerical questions from the measurement half of the paper. Between them they've filled Q3(b)/Q4(a)/Q5(b) in almost every semester.

Statistics on a data set — mean → deviation → std. deviation

4/7 semesters · 5 instances

Same five calculations — arithmetic mean, deviation of each value, algebraic sum of deviations, average deviation, standard deviation (sometimes + variance) — dressed up as raw lettered data, voltage readings, or precision-resistor calibration. The story changes; the formulas never do. Spring 2025 alone used it twice (both halves of Q3(b)).

Voltage measurement error propagation (V₁±%, V₂±%)

3/7 semesters
22S23S23A24S24A25S25A

Given two voltages with a tolerance percentage each, find the max % error of each and the max % error in their difference. V₁=100V±1%, V₂=80V±5% is the exact pair reused in both Spring 2024 and Autumn 2024.

Copper-constantan thermocouple, c = 3.75×10⁻² mV/°C and k = 4.5×10⁻⁵ mV/°C² — these two constants have been identical every single time this has appeared. Only T₁ moves (usually 1000°C). It sat out Autumn 2025, which historically means it's due.

Single-measurement error (absolute / % / relative accuracy)

1/7 — but it's the newest
22S23S23A24S24A25S25A

Expected value vs. measured value → absolute error, percentage error, relative accuracy, percentage accuracy. Brand-new format in Autumn 2025. Either it's the new house style for this slot, or a one-off simpler cousin of the Tier-2 voltage-error question. Worth knowing both.

Methodology: every question across the 7 uploaded papers was read and classified as either a calculation (given numeric data → compute a result) or a derivation/proof. Frequencies are counted per semester regardless of whether a problem appeared in the main slot or the OR alternative.
